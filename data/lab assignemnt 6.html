b. Why is standardization required for ridge and lasso regression, when for OLS it changed only how we interpret the coefficients, but not the fit of the model? Ridge and Lasso use standardization because both methods penalize the coefficient size but not affect the predictions. While OLS minimizes the prediction error but does not affect the coefficient size. So, standardization affects the coefficients in OLS, but does not affect the model's predictions.
